Voltage at the worst node on the feeder

statutory band ±6 %

Peak kilowatts are the headline, but voltage is what actually fails first on Indian LV feeders. The safety layer treats the lower band as a hard constraint, not a penalty term.

The forecast the controller was given

day-ahead, issued at 12:00

The optimiser never sees a single number. It sizes import headroom from the upper demand quantile and the lower solar quantile, so the width of these bands is a safety input — which is why the bands are calibrated rather than merely sharp.

Did the clever model earn its place?

chronological hold-out, 7 unseen days

Baselines first. A model only ships if it beats causal baselines on a quantile score and its stated intervals hold at every lead time. Every feature is built from an explicit forecast issue time, and weather inputs are issue-time forecasts, never realised weather. Raw quantile regression under-covers; conformal widening on a later calibration block repairs it, but coverage is measured, not assumed.
