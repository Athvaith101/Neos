How much capability does the neighbourhood have right now?

The Flexibility Envelope reads state the control and safety layers already computed — the controller's own import headroom, the calibrated forecast quantiles, and the exact device limits the dispatcher respects — and combines them into one number per control step.

ⓘThis is always an estimate, never a guarantee. It can be revised at the next replan, and it is bounded by the same forecast uncertainty and reserve floor the optimiser itself respects.
