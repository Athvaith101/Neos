● NEOS · Neighbourhood Energy Operating System

Make the neighbourhood flexible — without making people change their lives.

NEOS coordinates EVs, rooftop solar, batteries and flexible loads around a distribution transformer. Forecast uncertainty is carried into optimisation, every proposed action is projected through a safety layer, and the final action is verified against an unbalanced AC digital twin.

Research controls and optimisation details

what is actually inside the engine
Forecasting

Uncertainty-aware prediction

Causal demand forecasting, P10/P50/P90 distributions, issue-time synthetic weather, conformal calibration, forecast benchmarking and an EV information barrier.

Optimisation

Constraint-aware scheduling

Battery, EV and deferrable-load scheduling with hard EV requirements, device power/energy bounds, exact energy accounting and replanning.

Safety

Physics before action

Transformer loading, phase-resolved voltage, line ampacity, convergence/finiteness checks, import/export direction handling and final-action AC re-solving.

Flexibility

Flexibility Envelope

Estimates transformer headroom, forecast uncertainty, EV flexibility, battery flexibility, deferrable-load flexibility, protected reserve, duration and confidence.

Reserve

Protected resilience

Reserve floor, uncertainty-aware reserve logic, reserve-aware flexibility and outage/resilience state are integrated into the architecture.

Audit

Reproducible evidence

Paired experiments, multiple replicates, 95% confidence intervals, held-out neighbourhood populations, digital-twin calibration, energy audit and SHA-256 provenance.

What the system does — and does not — claim

research integrity
Verified core

Urban / suburban feeder

Strongest quantitatively validated part of NEOS: OpenDSS-verified feeder simulations covering evening transformer overload, EV surge and PV variability.

Simulation module

Low-income / peri-urban

Community Energy Hub, shared battery, essential-service prioritisation, outage/resilience and affordability/service concepts. Evidence is not yet equivalent to the urban benchmark.

Simulation module

Rural

Shared solar, battery, pumps, water, thermal/cold storage, productive loads, public lighting, priority tiers and surplus routing. Benchmark validation is pending.

Concept only

Islanding

Simulation/concept state machine only. This is not a certified protection design or public-feeder islanding claim.

Not implemented

Hardware control

No physical actuator control, hardware-in-the-loop, real-feeder deployment or DISCOM approval is claimed.

Safety boundary

Flexibility is not a guarantee

The flexibility envelope represents estimated available flexibility, not a contractual delivery guarantee.
